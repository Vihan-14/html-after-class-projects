pizza

Italian pizza, originating from Naples, is a culinary icon known for its simple, high-quality ingredients, traditional preparation, and cultural significance. Origins and History Italian pizza traces its roots to Naples in the 18th century, where it began as a simple dish for the poor, made with flatbread, tomatoes, cheese, and olive oil. The Marinara pizza, created in 1734, is the oldest tomato-based pizza, named for its popularity among fishermen. The first pizzeria, Pizzeria Port’Alba, opened in 1738 in Naples and still operates today, preserving traditional pizza-making methods. The word “pizza” was first documented in 997 AD, derived from the Latin “pinsa,” meaning “to flatten out”.
